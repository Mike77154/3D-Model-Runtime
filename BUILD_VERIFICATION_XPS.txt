model_runtime89 XPS geometry fix verification

Root cause:
- xps_mesh_info at on_mesh_begin does not yet contain vertex_count/face_count.
- provider_xps89 cached that incomplete structure and never refreshed it at on_mesh_end.
- skeleton callbacks remained valid, so rig-only display was possible while triangle_count stayed zero.

Fix:
- keep first_texture/first_vertex/first_face offsets from on_mesh_begin.
- overwrite cached xps_mesh_info from the final structure received by on_mesh_end.
